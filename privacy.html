A CLEAR LITTLE NOTE

Demo, data & privacy.

Here’s what this idea-stage storefront does—and what it doesn’t.

No real payments.

The payment choices and checkout are a visual test. Nothing charges a card, connects to UPI, ships a product or creates a real order.

Anonymous interest counts, only if you opt in.

If you choose “Allow anonymous counts,” a random ID is stored in this browser. The RIVORA demo database counts unique visits, product views, bag additions and demo checkouts. It does not record your name, email, phone, address, payment details or IP address.

Choose again any time.

Choose “No thanks” to keep analytics off. The button below resets your choice and shows the opt-in again.

Demo contact details.

hello@rivora.example and +91 90000 00000 are example values and aren’t monitored. Replace them with official details before the business launches.

Presentation project, not a finished store.

Product descriptions and prices are indicative concept content. A live business will need confirmed products, final prices, delivery and returns information, a real payment integration and its own legal and privacy review.
